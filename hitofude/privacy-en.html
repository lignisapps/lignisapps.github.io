Privacy Policy

SwitchTrail (iPhone app) / Effective: November 16, 2026

KLADO ("we") explains here how the iPhone app "SwitchTrail" ("the app") handles your information.

1. Information the app stores

Cleared levels, the days you solved the Daily Puzzle, and settings such as sound and haptics are stored only on your iPhone. We never send them to our servers. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes this information.

2. Checking the release date

To decide the free period (until December 13, 2026, Japan time; or one week after release if released on or after December 14, 2026), the app reads its own release date from Apple's public iTunes lookup service. Only the app's ID is sent; no information about you is sent.

3. Information collected for ads

The app shows ads with Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, only to people who installed the app after the free period and have not bought Remove Ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and app-specific identifiers
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Usage such as ads shown and how you interact with them
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

We do not share your progress with advertisers. You can change tracking permission at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The app works fully without it.

How Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services / Google Privacy Policy

4. Purchases

Remove Ads is processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

5. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
