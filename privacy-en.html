Privacy Policy

Alcohol Diary (iPhone app) · Effective November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Lignis Apps (“we”) handles information in the iPhone app “Alcohol Diary” (Japanese name: 純アルコール手帳, “the app”). If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the app stores

Your drinking entries (date and time, drink name, volume, ABV, number of drinks), the drinks you register and your settings (weekly limit, sex, weight, start of the day, reminder time, language) are stored only on your iPhone, in the area used by the app and its widget. We never send them to our servers, and there is no account.

Deleting the app deletes this information.

2. Information collected for ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (no ads after you buy Diary Plus). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you choose “Allow” for tracking) and app-specific identifiers
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Usage data such as the ads shown and your interactions with them
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

We never pass what you enter in the app, such as your drinking entries or weight, to anyone for ads. Alcohol ads are turned off.

You can change the tracking permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Every feature of the app works without it.

How Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services · Google Privacy Policy

3. Purchases

Diary Plus is purchased through Apple’s App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

4. Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your iPhone and do not send any information outside it.

5. Children

The app is for logging alcohol, so it is intended only for adults of legal drinking age where they live.

6. Changes

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page.
