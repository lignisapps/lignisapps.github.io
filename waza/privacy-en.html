Privacy Policy

Waza Compare (iPhone app) / Effective: November 11, 2026 · Updated November 2026: operator name and contact changed to KLADO

This policy explains how KLADO (“we”) handles information in the iPhone app “Waza Compare” (the “App”).

1. Information the App handles

Videos you import or record, the body skeleton data estimated from them, video names, saved sets, sync points and other settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes them.

The camera and microphone are used only to record a video when you tap Record. The App saves to Photos only when you choose “Save Video” in the share sheet.

2. Sending information

We never send your videos, skeleton data or names to us or to third parties. Skeleton estimation runs on your iPhone. There is no account. When you choose to share, the video goes only to the destination you pick.

3. Information collected for ads

The App shows an ad from Google AdMob (Google LLC) at the bottom of the video list (not shown if you have Plus or installed by December 13, 2026). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and app-specific identifiers
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Usage data such as ads shown and interactions
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Your videos and skeleton data are never given to advertisers. You can change tracking permission any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The App works fully without it.

How Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services / Google Privacy Policy

4. Purchases

Purchases of Plus are processed by Apple’s App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

5. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
