Waza Compare

An iPhone app to compare martial arts technique videos side by side, overlaid and in slow motion　日本語

Import videos from Photos or record one, then play 2–4 videos side by side or overlay two of them. Use slow motion, frame stepping and Sync to compare the same moment, and see the difference with skeleton lines and a 3D view. You can turn the comparison into a video and share it. Videos and skeleton analysis stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

Skeleton lines, 3D, comparing up to 4 videos, syncing and sharing are free. Plus (one-time purchase) lets you keep 5 or more videos (free: up to 4), save layouts as sets, remove the “Waza Compare” mark from shared videos, and remove the ad on the video list (tap “Plus” at the top left of the list). If you installed by December 13, 2026, everything is free for you. After changing or reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchase”.

FAQ

No skeleton lines appear.
Film the whole body (head to feet) in a bright place. Right after importing, keep the app open while it shows “Analyzing skeleton”. If it stops, reopen the app and it will continue.

No 3D skeleton appears.
3D is estimated by on-device AI and may not be available on some models (iOS 18 or later required). It is an estimate, not a precise measurement.

The skeleton shows a different person.
On the compare screen, tap “Choose” at the top left of the video, then tap the person you want.

I want to compare from the same moment.
Tap “Sync” at the top right of the compare screen and line up each video with its slider or the play and frame buttons.

I want to compare reps within one video.
Select just one video in the list, then tap “Compare reps within this video”.

Rename or delete a video.
Swipe left on it in the list, or long-press it.
