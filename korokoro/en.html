Marble Junction

Roll each marble
to its color.

Glass marbles keep rolling down a wooden track. Flip the switches at each fork and send every marble into the cup of its color. Flip one, and the path of every other marble changes too.

FEATURES

Features

  • 🔀

    Flip and sort

    Marbles roll on their own. Flip the switches to send each one to the cup of the same color. Three misses and you're out.

  • 🎚

    Easy and Hard

    Easy: tap toward a cup and the whole path lines up. Hard: flip one switch at a time. Clear a stage both ways for a crown.

  • 🪜

    30 stages

    Two colors first, then two switches and three colors, then three switches and four colors. Every stage is tuned to human reaction time.

  • ♾

    Endless

    No limit. It keeps speeding up until three misses. Pick a board and beat your best count and time.

FAQ

FAQ

It's too fast for me

Try Easy (Tap a color) first. Just tap toward the cup you want, and the whole path lines up.

Where do I tap when there are two or more switches?

On Hard, each switch has a dotted box around it. Tap the box to flip just that switch.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Open Settings (gear icon at the top right of the home screen) and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.
